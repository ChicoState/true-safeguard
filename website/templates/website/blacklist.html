Call of Duty series

High Risk
Games Teens

Platform: Various - Warnings: Violence, Blood and Gore, Strong Language, Mature Themes, Online Play

Why It's Flagged

  • Intense violence and combat scenarios
  • Online multiplayer with unmoderated chat
  • Strong language from other players
  • Military and graphic warfare themes

Tips for Parents

  • Use parental controls on online features
  • Disable voice chat or limit to friends only
  • Supervise online interactions
  • Discuss appropriate gaming behavior

Snapchat

High Risk
Social Media Middle School

Warnings: Privacy concerns, Disappearing messages, Snapstreaks pressure

Why It's Flagged

  • Disappearing messages encourage sharing without accountability
  • Snapstreaks create anxiety and pressure
  • Location sharing features
  • Inappropriate content sharing with peers

Tips for Parents

  • Review privacy settings regularly
  • Discuss appropriate sharing practices
  • Monitor for signs of social pressure
  • Use app monitoring tools

Instagram

High Risk
Social Media Teens

Warnings: Body image issues, Cyberbullying, Excessive screen time

Why It's Flagged

  • Comparison and unrealistic body standards
  • Cyberbullying through comments and DMs
  • Highly addictive algorithm design
  • Exposure to inappropriate content

Tips for Parents

  • Limit daily usage time
  • Discuss healthy social media habits
  • Review follower lists and content regularly
  • Enable restricted accounts and parental controls
